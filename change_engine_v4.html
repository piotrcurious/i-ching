The yarrow model is not a claim that every physical division is equiprobable. It is the standard idealized probability model for the 49-stalk procedure. Under it, the primary 64 figures are equiprobable, but the resulting figures are not: each resulting line is yin with probability 5/8. This is a property of the sampling distribution, not an interpretive ranking or claim of greater accuracy.

07 / CORPUS AND METHODWAITING

Changing-line arithmetic. Old yin (6) changes to yang; young yang (7) remains yang; young yin (8) remains yin; old yang (9) changes to yin. The six values are generated from bottom line to top line.

Coin method. Three independent fair coins yield 6, 7, 8, 9 with probabilities 1:3:3:1. Yarrow probability model. Three symbolic changes remove 5 or 9 stalks (3/4 or 1/4), then 4 or 8 twice (1/2 each). The remaining multiple of four yields 6, 7, 8 or 9 with probabilities 1:5:7:3 out of 16. This is probability-equivalent, not a physical hand-splitting simulation of 49 stalks.

Structural relations. Nuclear: lines 2–3–4 below and 3–4–5 above. Inverse: reverse the six line positions. Opposite: complement every line. These are distinct operations and are derived directly from the binary pattern.

The original Zhouyi does not provide a single universal instruction for choosing among several changing lines. The optional Zhu Xi convention is a later Song-dynasty systematization (1186); its two-line and four-line rules are acknowledged extrapolations. The neutral comparison mode displays every moving line without imposing a hierarchy. For Qian and Kun, the corpus also includes the special 用九 and 用六 texts.

Offline operation

The first launch retrieves the source corpus and caches it in browser storage. Once loaded, the reading engine works offline in the same browser. Use “EXPORT CORPUS JSON” to keep a portable copy, or import that JSON into another browser. For a genuinely self-contained copy with no first-launch network requirement, use “Export offline HTML” after the corpus has loaded.
