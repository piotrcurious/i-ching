How the lines are made

The number assigned to each throw matters: it preserves the distinction between a line at rest and a line in transition.

Three-coin method

Each coin contributes 2 or 3. Heads is assigned 3 and tails 2 in this implementation. Their sum gives one of four line values. Six throws form the hexagram, beginning at the bottom.

How to read the transformation

First identify the primary hexagram from all six lines. Every 6 or 9 then reverses: a changing yin becomes yang, and a changing yang becomes yin. The altered six lines form the relating hexagram.

Line positions are counted upward from 1 at the bottom to 6 at the top. The three lower lines form one trigram; the three upper lines form the other.

About this implementation

The 64 structural records use the received King Wen order, Unicode hexagram glyphs, and bottom-up binary patterns. The original text in this tool is a compact reflective layer written for this interface; it is not a substitute for studying different translations and commentarial traditions.

Random results are generated locally in the browser using the Web Crypto API where available. No question or casting data is sent to a server.
